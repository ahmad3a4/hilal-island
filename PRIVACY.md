# Hilal privacy policy

*Last updated: November 9, 2026*

Hilal is a Dynamic Island for Windows. It runs on your PC and does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What Hilal does not do

- No account, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Nothing about you, your PC or how you use Hilal is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What stays on your PC

These are kept only in Hilal's settings folder (`%APPDATA%\Hilal`) and never leave your PC:

- **Settings:** your city or coordinates, prayer-time method, azan and reminder choices.
- **Downloads:** the list of files you downloaded with Hilal.
- **Play time:** how long you played each game.
- **Logs:** an activity log and an error log, to help fix problems. They are only shared if you attach them to an issue yourself.

## When Hilal uses the internet

- **Downloads you start.** Hilal downloads a link from the website it points to, only when you ask.
- **Update check (GitHub version only).** Once a day, Hilal asks GitHub whether there is a new version. Nothing about you is sent, and you can turn this off in Settings. The Microsoft Store version doesn't do this, because the Store installs updates.

Prayer times are calculated on your PC, without the internet.

## Location

If you press **Detect** in Settings → Location, Hilal asks Windows for your position once, to work out prayer times. It is saved in your settings and not sent anywhere. You can pick a city instead and never share your location.

## Microphone, keyboard and other devices

- **Microphone:** Hilal can mute and unmute your microphone, but it never records or listens to it.
- **Keyboard:** it watches only the volume keys, your mute shortcut, Caps Lock and the typing language, to show them on the island. It never records what you type.
- **Devices, games and media:** battery levels of Bluetooth devices, the FPS of games and the song that's playing are read only to show them on the island, and are not stored or sent.

## Children

Hilal does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page.
